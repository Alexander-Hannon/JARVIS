# Missions
A mission is a standing goal JARVIS works on in the background: "check my inbox every two hours for anything from my
professors", "every Sunday at 6 PM, plan my week".

Each mission has a schedule, the apps it may use, whether it may propose changes, a per-run and monthly budget, and
quiet hours. Quiet missions only notify you when they find something. Results arrive in Notifications (and Telegram, if
paired). Missions read untrusted content like email, so their proposals only use rules you opted into missions.

Daily Command, Nightly Debrief and the Weekly War Room are built-in briefings shown on the same page.
