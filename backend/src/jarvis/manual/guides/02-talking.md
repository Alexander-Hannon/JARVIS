# Talking to JARVIS
Say it the way you'd say it to a person. One message can hold several requests.

| You say | JARVIS does |
|---|---|
| "What's on tomorrow and will it rain?" | Reads your calendar and the forecast, shows cards |
| "Add a task to email Dr. Lee Friday" | Prepares a Todoist task for your approval |
| "Block two hours tomorrow morning for thesis writing" | Finds a free slot, prepares a calendar block |
| "Anything in my inbox I need to answer?" | Triages email: needs reply, waiting, FYI |
| "Draft a reply saying I'll have grades by Friday" | Saves a draft in your mailbox after approval; never sends on its own |
| "Turn the office lights to 60%" | Prepares a Home Assistant action (lights can be automated by a rule) |
| "Analyze this CSV and chart spending by month" | Runs Python in the sandbox and shows the result |
| "Remember that I prefer morning workouts" | Saves it to memory (only when you say remember) |
| "How do I connect Slack?" | Answers from this manual |

Dates come only from your own words: "tomorrow" means the day after you said it, even if JARVIS processes it later.
