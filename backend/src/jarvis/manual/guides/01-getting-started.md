# Getting started
JARVIS is your personal operator. Talk to it on the Command page (hold the orb or type), from Telegram, or let it work in
the background through missions.

**What it does on its own:** reads your calendar, tasks, email, notes and connected apps; researches the web; runs code
in its sandbox; answers.

**What it never does on its own:** change anything outside JARVIS. Every change becomes an approval card first, unless
you created a rule that approves that kind of low-risk change for you (see *Approvals and autonomy*).

First steps:
1. Settings → Budget: choose a budget and turn on the reasoning model.
2. Connections: connect Todoist and Google Calendar, then email and any apps you want.
3. Settings → Local files: add a workspace folder (and index your notes vault into search).
4. Try: "What does my day look like?" or "Plan my week."
