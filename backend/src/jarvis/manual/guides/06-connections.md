# Connections
Each app gets only what you grant in Connections.

- **Todoist, Google Calendar:** tasks and personal calendar blocks (no invitations).
- **Gmail, Outlook:** triage, read, drafts. Sending is off until you allow it per mailbox, and each send still asks.
- **Notion:** search and read pages you share with the integration; add notes under pages you pick.
- **Slack:** mentions and search; notes to your own DM; channel posts only where you allow and always with approval.
- **Canvas:** assignments, to-dos, announcements (read-only).
- **Spotify, Home Assistant:** music and smart home. Locks, alarms and garage doors always ask.
- **Travel time, bank balances (SimpleFIN):** read-only.
- **MCP servers:** plug in any MCP server. Each tool is off until you review it; if its tools change, JARVIS stops using
  it until you look again.
- **Telegram:** talk to JARVIS from your phone; pair your chat with a one-time code.
